# Chinese Group Report Format

Follow a user-provided structure exactly. If no structure is supplied, use this template.

# 专题论文周报：{专题}

> 统计区间：{YYYY-MM-DD} 至 {YYYY-MM-DD}（含首尾日期）

## 一、本期专题概览

Use 5–8 bullets rather than a continuous paragraph. Begin every bullet with a short bold dimension label and keep it to 1–2 concise sentences. Cover only dimensions supported by the verified paper pool. A typical structure is:

- **Tabular Foundation Models**：概括新的预训练、上下文学习或跨表泛化范式。
- **TabPFN 及扩展**：指出代表性扩展、适用边界与重要变化。
- **Time Series Foundation Models**：总结零样本、少样本与跨领域预测进展。
- **Structured Data**：说明不同表结构、特征空间、缺失值或分布迁移的处理方式。
- **LLM 与表格推理**：概括 LLM 在理解、推理、分析或 agent 化方面的角色变化。
- **因果发现与推荐**：如有充分证据，说明是否出现更通用的基础模型范式；否则明确本期高质量工作较少。
- **组内重点关注**：点名最值得精读的工作及其与组内方向的联系。

## 三、重点论文精读推荐

Choose exactly 3 papers from section two. Use one paragraph per paper to cover why it matters, the key problem, pretraining/in-context/adaptation method, generalization evidence, strengths and limitations versus relevant baselines, and implications for the group's work.

## 四、关键词趋势

List 10–15 keywords. Give one short Chinese sentence for why each is receiving attention. Base the list on the verified candidate pool and optionally the previous three months when requested.

## 五、组内讨论问题

Provide 5 concrete questions connecting the topic to the group's research interests.

## 六、检索说明

State:

- Inclusive date range
- Sources searched
- Number of candidates reviewed and papers selected
- Whether older exceptions were included
- Any important coverage or verification limitations

Do not add an appendix of unverified search results unless requested.
